# Boxbuch: QR-Vorratsliste für Vakuumboxen nach dem Aus der ZWILLING-App

**Stage:** killed · **Platform:** pwa · **Score:** 3.5/5 · **Spend:** $0.86

For owners of FRESH & SAVE vacuum boxes and bags who lose their inventory and shelf-life reminders when the ZWILLING app shuts down on 2026-12-31. It is an offline PWA with no account that scans the QR codes already on their lids and bags, stores the contents and the date sealed on the phone, and exports reminders as a calendar file.

**Why now:** ZWILLING retires its app on 2026-12-31. FRESH & SAVE inventory, reminders and QR/NFC scanning stop, and the company says no alternative exists. The Android listing carried the retirement notice by 2026-10-23. (2026-10-23). Window: About 7 weeks until the shutdown. Searches for a replacement should peak in December 2026 and January 2027 and then fade as owners give up on the codes. ZWILLING says it plans no replacement, so a first mover owns the queries until owners stop caring.

## Alternatives found

- ZWILLING App (abandoned): Shuts down 2026-12-31 with no export and no successor.
- Lomi Labels (partial): iOS only and built around its own labels; no sign it reads FRESH & SAVE codes.
- Stökk (partial): Pantry app with expiry alerts and check-in by scan or by hand; no QR labels on containers (per search summary, not opened).
- Fridge Assistant (Home Assistant integration) (partial): A DIY setup that needs Home Assistant and its own printed labels; not something a typical cook would set up.
- Mein Inventar (QR box app) (abandoned): Per iphone-ticker, the developer has been silent for four years, and more than three boxes require in-app purchases.
